A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we print.

Can I bring my own design?+

Yes. Send your design to HIGP and tell us the size you need. We can discuss whether the file is suitable for printing or needs layout adjustments.

How do I get a price?+

Message us with your print type, dimensions, quantity, material preference, and deadline. Pricing depends on the details of your project.

Can I order through this website?+

Online ordering is still being built. For now, contact HIGP on Facebook to discuss and confirm your print. The workspace is an interactive preview.
